## How the savings estimate works

Excess for an event is the sum over its flagged days of max(actual - baseline, 0) on total spend. The baseline is causal (trailing 21-day median of de-seasonalised spend, times the weekday and month-end factors), so sustained issues are only counted while they are flagged. That makes the estimate conservative for level shifts and slow leaks, which the capture ratio above shows.
